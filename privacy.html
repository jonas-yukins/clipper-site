Privacy notice

Updated November 7, 2026. This notice covers the Clipper waitlist and Clipper's use of YouTube API Services.

What we collect

If you join the waitlist, we collect your email address. That is the only thing the form asks for.

Why we collect it

We use it to tell you when Clipper early access is available to you, and to send occasional updates about the product. We do not sell it, share it for advertising, or use it for anything else.

Where it is stored

Waitlist sign-ups are stored in a database hosted by Supabase (a cloud database provider), which processes them on our behalf. We also record the date you signed up and which page you signed up from. We keep your email until you ask us to remove it or until the waitlist is closed.

YouTube API Services

Clipper uses YouTube API Services to upload short clips to YouTube channels that have connected Clipper, and to read those videos' statistics. By connecting a YouTube channel to Clipper, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

When you connect a channel, with your permission Clipper:

  • Accesses: the ability to upload videos to your channel, and read-only access to your channel's videos, their public statistics (views, likes, comments) and their YouTube Analytics (average view duration and percentage viewed).
  • Collects and stores: the IDs and titles of the videos Clipper uploaded for you, and the statistics above for those videos, so we can show how clips perform and improve which moments we pick. The access token Google gives us is stored encrypted and never shared.
  • Uses: this data only to upload your clips and to report and improve their performance. We do not use it for advertising.
  • Shares: nothing. We do not sell, rent or share YouTube data with third parties. Our hosting providers (Amazon Web Services) store it on our behalf.

Revoking access: you can disconnect Clipper at any time on the Google security settings page. Clipper then stops all access to your channel.

Deleting your data: after you revoke access, or when you ask us at clipper.headq@gmail.com, we delete the YouTube data we stored about your channel within 30 days. Video files we generate are deleted from our storage 30 days after they are made.

Clipper does not place cookies or similar technologies on your device in connection with YouTube API Services.

This website

  • This site does not use cookies, analytics or tracking scripts.
  • It is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical request data, such as IP addresses, to operate and secure its service.

Removing your email

Every email we send includes a way to unsubscribe. You can also email clipper.headq@gmail.com (or reply to any email from us) and ask us to delete your address, and we will remove it from the waitlist.

Contact

Questions about this notice or your data: clipper.headq@gmail.com.
