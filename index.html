How it works

One link in, a set of vertical clips out. No timeline editing.

  1. 1

    Paste a link

    Drop in the URL of a long YouTube video or a Twitch stream VOD.

  2. 2

    Clipper watches the whole thing

    It transcribes the speech, captions what is on screen with a vision model, and detects audio spikes and chat bursts.

  3. 3

    Moments are proposed and judged

    An AI curator proposes moments. A separate AI judge scores each one and trims the edges so a clip never cuts before the punchline.

  4. 4

    Clips land in Discord

    Finished vertical clips, titles and hashtags are delivered to your Discord. Direct posting to TikTok, Shorts and Reels is planned.

What you get in each clip

Every clip is rendered at 9:16 and ready to post.

  • Automatic reframing

    Each shot is reframed for vertical. For streams, a face-cam-on-top, gameplay-below layout.

  • Word-by-word captions

    Animated captions in bold uppercase with an outline, and the spoken word highlighted as it is said.

  • Punch-in zooms and sound effects

    Punch-in zooms and sound effects are added during the edit to emphasise key lines.

  • Hook title

    A short on-screen title in the opening seconds that sets up the moment.

  • Titles and hashtags

    A post title and hashtags are generated for each clip, so it is ready to upload.

  • Multi-part clips

    The curator can splice several parts of a video into one clip when a story is spread out.

  • Clean edges

    The judge adjusts where each clip starts and ends, so it does not open cold or stop before the payoff.

  • Delivery

    Clips are sent to Discord today. Planned Direct posting to TikTok, YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels.

How we pick moments

Clipper does not pick clips from one signal. It reads the video several ways, then uses two separate AI passes: one to propose and one to judge.

  1. 1 · Signals

    Read the whole video

    • Speech: transcribed with word-level timestamps, which also drive the captions.
    • Picture: a vision model writes short captions of what is on screen, scene by scene.
    • Audio: sudden spikes in loudness are detected.
    • Chat: for streams, bursts of chat activity are lined up against the video.

  2. 2 · Curator

    Propose candidate moments

    The signals are merged into one time-aligned script of the video. An AI curator reads it and proposes moments, including multi-part clips that splice several parts of the video together.

  3. 3 · Judge

    Score and trim each one

    A separate AI judge scores every candidate on five criteria:

    • Hook
    • Payoff
    • Coherence
    • Emotional pull
    • Trend fit

    It also trims the in and out points so a clip never cuts before the punchline.

  4. 4 · Edit and render

    Build the vertical clip

    Faces and on-screen elements are detected to reframe each shot. Captions, zooms, sound effects and the hook title are added, and the clip is rendered at 9:16.

Built on

  • Transcription: word-level speech-to-text (Whisper, with a hosted speech API option)
  • Language and vision models: Anthropic Claude for scene captions, curation and judging
  • Reframing: MediaPipe face and object detection
  • Rendering: FFmpeg and Remotion
  • Infrastructure: built to run its heavy stages on GPU instances on AWS (Amazon EC2 and S3), which we are moving to now
